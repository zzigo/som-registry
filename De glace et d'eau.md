# De glace et d'eau

iii residency (02.2026–03.2026, in development). A "sonic ecofiction" installation reflecting on the decay of glacial landscapes, mediated through an audio augmented-reality experience. Robin Koek is a sound artist/designer (lecturer at the Royal Conservatoire The Hague; co-founder of the platform Environments) exploring listening as more-than-human empathy and "acoustic justice." Jean-Emmanuel Rosnet works on musical composition, field recording, sound collage and sonification, focused on orality and territorial sound traces in the Anthropocene. During the residency they develop wearables and exhibition furniture, and record the English narration of the original French text by writer Fanny Taillandier, processing sounds from their expedition on the Argentière Glacier. Supported by Creative Fund NL; developed with residency support of Pléiades and Césaré; scientific advice from the Institute of Geosciences of the Environment (IGE).

SOM note: highest fiction-load of the set — the work explicitly builds a fictional/ecological world (ecofiction); the "instrument" is an environment-scale listening apparatus.
