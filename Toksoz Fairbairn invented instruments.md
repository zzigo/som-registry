# Toksöz Fairbairn — invented-instruments series

Performer, sound artist and scholar working around the edges of installation, improvisation, composition and craft. Has built traditional, experimental and invented instruments for decades; currently presents concerts and installations with a series of hybrid acoustic instruments developed and constructed over the last five years. Regular guest of Klangforum Wien and Talea Ensemble; founding member of the experimental chamber groups hoodwink (with Winnie Huang), Blechtrommel (with João Carlos Pacheco) and les Trombones de Bâle. Author of the book *dis/cord: Thinking Sound through Agential Realism* (Punctum Books, 2022).

SOM note: the contemporary inheritor of the maker-performer (luthier-composer) figure — useful contrast case where object remains present and central, low fiction-load, high craft.
