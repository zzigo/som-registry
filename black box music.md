1. **Theatrical Setup**:
    
    - The centerpiece of the performance is a **black box**, which the conductor manipulates with their hands to produce sound and visual effects. This "box" becomes an instrument in itself.
    - The conductor, usually positioned at the front, uses hand gestures to "play" the box, creating both acoustic and amplified sounds.
2. **Integration of Video**:
    
    - The piece uses **real-time video projection**, showing close-ups of the conductor's hands inside the box.
    - This visual component adds an intimate, almost surreal perspective, turning the audience's focus to the physical actions and their sonic outcomes.
3. **Collaboration Between Sound and Action**:
    
    - The composition blurs the line between music and performance art. The conductor's physical gestures, amplified by microphones and visualized through video, generate the work’s core material.
4. **Complex Ensemble Writing**:
    
    - The performers in the ensemble interact with the sounds and visuals, creating a layered and immersive experience.
    - There’s a playful, almost absurd quality to how music and theatrical elements interact.
5. **Experimental Approach**:
    
    - Steen-Andersen incorporates everyday objects, unconventional techniques, and a sense of humor, reflecting his broader interest in exploring the boundaries of sound and performance.
