# Plastic Echoes

iii residency (02.2025–03.2025). An instrument that creates sound from interaction with microplastic particles using ultra-sonic sensing. Conventionally microplastics are detected by filtration and visual identification; Austen adapts recent sound-based detection methods — particle size is proportional to resonance wavelength — to directly sonify the presence/type/amount of microplastic in environmental samples, so the instrument "plays" the sample. Austen holds a PhD in Chemistry and is a Fellow of the Royal Society of Arts. Realized in part within the S+T+ARTS 4Water II residency programme (ADAPT Centre, Dublin City University, and Beta Festival), with support from Port of Galway, Galway Culture Company, Galway City Council, Culture Works and the EU S+T+ARTS programme.

SOM note: object present but its meaning is ecological/indexical — the instrument sonifies a pollutant; agency hybrid (human performer + material behaviour).
