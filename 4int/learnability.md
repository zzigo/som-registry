# Learnability

The temporal dimension of the performer-instrument relationship: how quickly and reliably a performer develops the sensorimotor schemes that make the instrument's expressive space accessible. Learnability is distinct from playability (which concerns the threshold) and expressivity (which concerns the ceiling) — it concerns the gradient between the two.

An instrument can be designed for low learnability intentionally (deep investment rewarded with deep expressivity) or high learnability (quick access, shallower ceiling). The choice is a compositional and design decision.
