# Osc Twins (+ DIY sound-instrument expansion toolkit)

**_Oscil Twins_** is an audio-visual instrument that utilizes the CRT screen of an oscilloscope as its display. The output signal generated by the device moves the electric beam within the oscilloscope, creating intricate and dynamic patterns. By routing the same signal into a sound system, we can hear tones that correspond to the visual imagery, creating a unique interplay between sound and seeing.  
  
Part of algorithms are inspired by Mitchell Waite’s _Oscilloscope Graphic Artist_, published in Popular Electronics in 1975, _Oscil Twins_ translates the _OGA_ circuit into software and reimagines this concept as an live instrument by integrating custom-developed sound patches in _Pure Data_ and _Linux_ system running on _Raspberry Pi_ to generate signal in real-time react to human controlled interface.

iii residency (2025). Sky Kung is a Hong Kong-based media artist, music producer and instrument maker (also guitarist in the band Lucid Express; solo ambient/tape-loop project "the black sea"). Osc Twins is an open-source instrument for live audio-visual performance using obsolete displays such as CRT oscilloscopes; during the residency he expands it with a further obsolete display (a VGA LCD monitor) and integrates it into an audiovisual performance. Paired with a DIY sound-instrument expansion toolkit — an open-source electronic system that transforms found objects (cabinet lighting strips, discarded metal enclosures) into playable electroacoustic instruments serving as additional sound/visual inputs. The work foregrounds how open-source resources let artists build their own tools. Supported by Contemporary Musiking Hong Kong (CMHK) and iii.
