# Photosynthesizer

iii residency (05.2025–09.2025). Generates sound from the electrical output of cyanobacteria during photosynthesis: the organism produces electrons under light, which are measured and transformed into photo-reactive soundscapes responsive to environmental light. Lena Kuzmich (b. 1998, Vienna) is a visual artist (moving image, bio-art, installation), co-founder of the collective Transformative Narratives. Scientific partners: Joshua Lawrence (Yusuf Hamied Dept. of Chemistry, Univ. of Cambridge), Lucia B. Giron (Dept. of Biochemistry, Univ. of Cambridge), Raoul Frese (Dept. of Physics and Astronomy, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam). Supported by the Creative Industries Fund NL. A co-authored paper documents the underlying technology and sound-generation process [expand: citation].

SOM note: agency is non-human (the organism plays); the human role shifts from performer to designer/cultivator — a strong case for the dematerialization-of-the-player axis rather than the object.
