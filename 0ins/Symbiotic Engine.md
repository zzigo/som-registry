# Symbiotic Engine — *Microbial Cosmologies*

A biofuel-powered "symbiotic engine," one of the speculative technologies proposed by the *Microbial Cosmologies* team within the RISD × Hyundai Motor Group research partnership (Research & Strategic Partnerships Program, via the Nature Lab; announced June 2020). The interdisciplinary group — Meredith Binnette ('20 FAV), Yimei Hu ('20 ID/JM), Danlei "Elena" Huang (MID '21), Georgina Nolan (MFA '20 GD), advised by Assistant Professor of Graphic Design Anastasiia Raina — built *Microbial Cosmologies*, a speculative model for a future shaped by human–microbe cooperation, yielding among other things new energy sources from microbial biofuels.

The brief was post-pandemic mobility: how to move freely while reducing fossil-fuel dependence. The team's answer reframes the engine not as a fossil-fuel combustion unit but as a cultivated symbiotic organism — a living energy partner rather than a machine.

SOM note: a boundary / contrast case for SOOG. The "instrument" is not a sounding object at all but a speculative *organ* in the broad organological sense — a working part of a living-machine assemblage. It sits at the maximum of the fiction axis (5): a fully projected future technology, agency distributed across human and microbial actors, object present only as render/proposal.
