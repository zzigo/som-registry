# GASP




iii commission, in development; premiere fall 2026. Diane Mahín (b. 1993) is a Dutch-Iranian performance maker and sociologist who builds performative worlds driven by sound and image, dissecting the body as a social artifact (she also makes work with her brother Manuel Groothuysen under the name FADAT). GASP turns to inhalation rather than exhalation as the sounding gesture: it traps five performers in a continuous inhale — unable to exhale, speak, or act — creating a vocal and physical world where everything enters the body but nothing is released. Developed with iii as part of the two-year New Makers Trajectory of the Dutch Performing Arts Fund (Fonds Podiumkunsten).

SOM note: strong link to the Karlsruhe thesis — the "instrument" is the breathing body itself; object dematerialized, agency human, feedback loop is respiratory. Inhalation inverts the canonical wind-instrument exhalation.
