# Lossouarn — sensory-expansion instrument

iii Encouragement Opportunity 2025 winner (formerly iii Residency Award, 5th edition), awarded to a fresh graduate of the Royal Academy of Art The Hague. Lossouarn graduated with an MA in ArtScience (KABK) with the tactile and sound installation "DE CŒUR EN CHŒURS" [Hearts in Chorus / From heart to heart], made of copper, marble, transducers and sound. She seeks to mediate attention, amplify imperceptible signals, and build instruments that expand sensory experience by engaging the invisible layers of information around us — reflecting on dependency on technology and its impact on perception, and asking how technology can act as a medium for resonance rather than rationalization and measurement.

SOM note: the instrument's function is perceptual prosthesis — making the imperceptible sensible; directly resonant with the feedback-as-perceptible-difference axiom in the Karlsruhe talk.
