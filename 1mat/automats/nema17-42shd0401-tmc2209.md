## NEMA 17 42SHD0401 + TMC2209

The NEMA 17 stepper is the canonical workhorse of open-loop precision positioning in musical robotics. Paired with the Trinamic TMC2209 driver in **StealthChop2** mode, it becomes nearly inaudible at speeds below 600 rpm — making it viable for acoustic performance contexts. The 200-step/rev resolution (1.8°) interpolated to 256 µsteps by the TMC2209 yields 0.007° per commanded pulse.

## Actuation Profile

```
MCU STEP/DIR (3.3V) → TMC2209 (StealthChop2) → 2-phase NEMA 17 → D-cut shaft
```

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Nominal voltage (coil) | 2.8 V (run at 12–24 V via chopper) |
| Holding torque | 440 mNm |
| Max radial load | 28 N |
| Microstep (TMC2209) | up to 256 (interpolated) |
| Noise (StealthChop2) | perceived 1/5 |

## Organological Notes

For musical robotics: use StealthChop2 at speeds ≤600 rpm (most organological actuation). For fast return moves between musical gestures, switch to SpreadCycle and accept brief tonal noise. Pair with AccelStepper (Arduino) or RPi.GPIO + custom step generator for RT control.
