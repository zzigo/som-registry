## NEMA 11 28SHD0204 + TMC2208

Compact NEMA 11 stepper (28 mm frame) paired with TMC2208. Half the weight and footprint of NEMA 17 at ~20% of the torque — ideal for light actuation embedded inside instrument bodies where mass and volume are constrained. StealthChop2 brings the acoustic noise floor below perception in most performance contexts.

## Actuation Profile

```
MCU STEP/DIR → TMC2208 (StealthChop2, 256µstep) → NEMA 11 → 4mm shaft
```

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Holding torque | 90 mNm |
| Weight | 95 g |
| Frame | 28×28 mm |
| Noise (StealthChop2) | perceived 1/5 |
