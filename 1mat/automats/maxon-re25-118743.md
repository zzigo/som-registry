## Maxon RE 25 118743

Maxon's RE (Rhombic Engraving) series coreless brushed DC motor. Zero cogging, 85% peak efficiency, Swiss precision bearings. Larger and more torque-capable than the Faulhaber 2230 — preferred when higher continuous torque (~33 mNm) is needed while maintaining the acoustic silence of a coreless design.
