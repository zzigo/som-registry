- any device that converts electrical energy to mechanical energy.
- the magnetic field of an activvated solenoid has positive and negative poles that will attract or repel material sensitive to magnets. 
- the electromagnetic field causes the piston to either move backward or forward, which is how motion is created by a solenoid coil.



Solenoids are essential components in various applications due to their ability to generate controlled magnetic fields and mechanical movement. They are commonly used in devices such as relays, valves, and actuators. When an electric current passes through the coil, it creates a magnetic field, which can be harnessed to perform work, such as moving a plunger or opening a valve.
